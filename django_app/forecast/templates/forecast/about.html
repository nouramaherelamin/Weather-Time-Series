About Atmos

A machine learning app that forecasts the next 7 days of mean temperature for {{ city }}.

How it works

  • {{ n_years }} years of hourly weather from the Open-Meteo archive API, cleaned (gaps, duplicates, impossible values, outliers) and aggregated to daily values.
  • Time-series EDA: trend, yearly seasonality, decomposition, ACF/PACF and stationarity tests (see the Task 4 & 5 notebooks).
  • For each of the next 7 days a separate model (Ridge, Gradient Boosting, Random Forest) predicts the mean temperature from today’s temperature, humidity, wind, pressure and the day of the year.
  • Validated chronologically on the last 90 days (never shuffled, no leakage). Models are compared against “persistence” and “seasonal average” baselines.
  • The “Why this prediction?” chart replaces each input by its average and shows how much the forecast moves.
